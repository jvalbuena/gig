## Job description (key requirements)
Area Architect providing architectural leadership across multiple agile teams,
aligning solutions to business strategy, enterprise standards, and security
requirements. Establishes architecture guardrails including AI development
standards/governance while enabling team innovation. Works with enterprise/
global/solution architects.
Required: extensive enterprise IT experience (financial services/insurance
preferred), architecture and solution design background; broad technical range
(cloud, infra, security, apps, databases, middleware, AI, emerging tech); ability
to define/guide technology strategy; FinOps, cost optimisation, forecasting,
financially efficient design; AI development/governance/AI SecOps/secure-by-design
knowledge; risk management, IT control frameworks, compliance in regulated
environments; strong stakeholder management, communication, leadership.
