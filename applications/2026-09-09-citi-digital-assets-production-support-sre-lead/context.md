## Job description (key requirements)
Own reliability and operational integrity of business-critical digital asset
platforms (blockchain-connected systems, digital wallets, high-throughput
transaction processing). Senior IC, hybrid (3 days office / 2 remote).
Required: 6-10 yrs production support/SRE/platform engineering on
high-availability mission-critical systems; incident management and
systems-level troubleshooting (Linux diagnostics, distributed debugging);
observability tooling (Splunk, Grafana, ELK, Prometheus, OTEL, or GCO);
Python/Java scripting and automation; strong SQL for diagnostics; leading
requirement-gathering/scoping across concurrent large programmes; strong
communicator translating technical issues to business impact. Beneficial:
markets tech/electronic trading, payments/FinTech exchanges, SLOs/error
budgets, blockchain/DLT/digital wallets in a regulated environment.
