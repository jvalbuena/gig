## Job description (key requirements)
Lead and mentor the Linux Product Team; own Linux platform configuration
across DroneSentry-C2 ecosystem and ruggedized field/embedded hardware; build
secure Linux images (server, edge, embedded); design observability/monitoring/
telemetry standards; L3 support for OS/system-level production issues;
establish reliability engineering practices and incident management.
Required: extensive Linux ops (Ubuntu, Debian, RHEL, Yocto); SRE principles;
team leadership; deep Linux internals (kernel, init systems, cgroups,
bootloaders); Bash + Python/Go; secure boot/TPM/OS-level security; custom
Linux image building; SLO/SLI + incident management; networking +
Kubernetes/containerization. Desirable: IaC/config management, embedded/
appliance products, edge/air-gapped environments, AWS/hybrid cloud, SBOM/
supply-chain security, defence/regulated background, open-source
contributions, developer platform/internal tooling. Reports to Director, AI
Systems & Platforms.
