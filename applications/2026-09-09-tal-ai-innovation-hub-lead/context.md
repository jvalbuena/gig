- **Date**: 2026-09-09   **Status**: drafted

## Job description (key requirements)
Player-coach lead for a 2-analyst AI automation team embedded in TAL's Individual
Life division. Hands-on building automation in Microsoft Power Platform (Power
Automate, AI Builder in particular) and Copilot Studio; runs discovery sessions
with business teams (underwriting, customer service, product, marketing,
distribution); owns end-to-end delivery pipeline across concurrent initiatives;
coaches/develops 2 analysts; manages senior business stakeholders; embeds AI
governance per TAL's AI Implementation Policy. Highly regarded: financial
services/insurance/regulated industry background, AI governance frameworks,
GenAI concepts (prompt engineering, RAG, AI Builder doc intelligence), Microsoft
certs (PL-200, PL-400, AI-900).
