# Macquarie Technology Group — Product Delivery Lead
- **Date**: 2026-08-25   **Status**: drafted
- **Source**: pasted

## Job description (key requirements)
- Hands-on Applications Product Owner / technical Business Analyst, owning a portfolio of
  internal apps (custom .NET, SaaS, microservices) inside an IT delivery squad.
- 5+ years recent hands-on BA/technical BA experience.
- Demonstrated Product Ownership: backlog prioritisation, roadmap shaping, value-based
  trade-off decisions in Agile.
- In depth use of AI tools (Claude, Copilot or similar) — explicitly central to
  how the team works.
- Experience working directly with software engineering teams across the SDLC.
- Technical fluency across custom software, SaaS, APIs, modern platforms.
- Strong stakeholder engagement/influence across all levels; balances strategic
  initiatives with BAU/operational responsibilities.
- Sydney-based, blended CBD/WFH.

## Positioning
"Principal Engineer · Technical Product Delivery & Solution Owner" — kept his real,
honest title (Principal Engineer) rather than claiming a BA/PO title, but reframed the
entire résumé around the product/delivery lens: requirements negotiation, roadmap-shaping,
stakeholder influence, and AI-tool fluency, rather than infra/observability depth. The OTEL
bank-wide standard story was reframed as a product-ownership case study (identify gap →
build case → win approval → negotiate requirements → ship adoption), since that's the
strongest true analog to "shaping a roadmap and making value-based trade-off decisions."

## Match map
- Strong: AI tool fluency (Claude Code/Copilot daily, genuine and current — a real
  differentiator here); technical fluency across APIs/SaaS/microservices/.NET exposure;
  stakeholder engagement and cross-team negotiation (OTEL standard adoption); balancing
  strategic initiatives vs BAU (Sales API reliability ownership + OTEL initiative
  simultaneously).
- Stretch: "Product Ownership" experience — reframed backlog/Scrum Master/agile ceremony
  experience and the OTEL initiative (which functioned like a roadmap-shaping, trade-off-
  making initiative) as the closest true analog. Real, but not literally backlog ownership
  of an app portfolio.
- Gap: No formal Business Analyst or Product Owner title — 15+ yr Principal Engineer
  applying to what reads as a 5–8 yr BA/PO-level role. Named directly and confidently in
  cover letter paragraph 3 rather than hidden ("I don't hold a formal BA/PO title... but
  the pattern of work is the same").
