- **Date**: 2026-09-10   **Status**: drafted

## Job description (key requirements)
Business-facing production support/SRE role for eTrading FICC electronic trading
platforms across APAC (Singapore, Japan, Hong Kong, Australia). Incident/problem
management for business-critical outages, DR planning/execution, patch management,
follow-the-sun support, early-APAC-hours + rotational weekend coverage. SME for
key eTrading applications. Required: 10+ yrs production support/SRE for
business-critical platforms; monitoring dashboards/KPIs; DR planning; leading
Incident/Problem calls and post-incident RCA; Unix, Python, shell scripting;
Oracle, PostgreSQL, Cassandra; Splunk, AppDynamics, Dynatrace, Grafana, ITRS
Geneos; Control-M, Autosys; AWS/GCP familiarity advantageous. Explicit required
qualification: demonstrated use of enterprise-authorized AI in production
operations workflows (validating AI-assisted recommendations, escalating when
uncertain, data sensitivity/security/auditability). Preferred: ITIL v4, AWS
certification (EC2, S3, EKS, RDS, CloudWatch).
